About the lab

The Intelligent Health Sciences Lab (IHS Lab) opened in August 2026 within the Master's Program in Medical Informatics at National Tsing Hua University, led by Assistant Professor Ching-Heng Lin.

We bring real hospital data and questions into the lab, from ECGs and medical images to clinical notes, and build AI and information systems that clinicians can understand and actually use.
